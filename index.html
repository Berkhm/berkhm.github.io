Publications

Robustness Is Not One Number: U-Net Variants for Pancreas CT Segmentation Under Three Clinical Image Degradations and Three Training-Data Levels

B. Hökmen (co-first author) et al. · ISAS 2026

  • Compared U-Net, Attention U-Net, U-Net++ and UNETR for pancreas and pancreatic tumor segmentation on the MSD Task07 dataset using PyTorch.
  • Trained and evaluated 12 models at 25%, 50% and 100% training-data levels to study data efficiency and robustness.
  • Assessed robustness under simulated CT slice thickening, low-dose noise and reduced soft-tissue contrast, including performance across tumor-size groups.
  • More training data improved pancreas segmentation for every architecture, with substantially larger gains under contrast degradation for U-Net and U-Net++.
